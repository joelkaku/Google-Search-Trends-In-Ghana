### GOOGLE SEARCH TRENDS ON AI AND DATA SCIENCE IN GHANA OVER THE LAST HALF DECADE
Hi there 👋!

Ever wondered how conversations around AI and Data Science have evolved in Ghana over the last half decade?

A dive into Google Trends focused on Ghana revealed an interesting picture of rapidly growing interest in these fields.   

![page 1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5a21c016-3fd8-4bd9-9812-985e541fa74c)


📈 While searches on Data Science remained fairly constant from 2020 till date, searches on AI rose steeply towards the end of 2022, burgeoning awareness and engagement with AI. This abruptly heightened AI interest coincides with the release of ChatGPT around November of 2022 which made waves globally.



📈The visuals show which cities these searches can be traced to and almost all searches occurred around major cities. 
Are our rural communities being left out of the AI boom?🤔  






![page 2](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/111c944f-df83-440d-a106-bbe67aa0ff78)




📈Across the various regions in the country, we see that the percentages of searches on AI far outweigh those on Data Science. My guess is, many of us aren't so much interested in the fundamentals, which is data science, as we are in the finished AI product.  


![page 3](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e2ea6002-fafb-45e2-a782-063568d18a68)


📈This successive visual gives a deeper insight into the specific search terms employed in these queries. On AI, we see terms like ai, chat ai, free ai, gemini, chatgpt, and a plethora of others. The search terms on Data Science revealed a vivid interest in data science careers, masters programs and training programs or courses. Thankfully a number of organizations in Ghana such as Blossom Academy and ALX GHANA are helping to bridge this training and expertise gap.


![page 4](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4dafc665-5dbe-4b22-80de-77e48c93de54)

📈This last visual came up out of sheer curiosity: I wanted to know what Ghanaians have been googling over the past week and unsurprisingly, majority of the searches centered around football with a handful being on current affairs, cars and finance.   



I believe this increased search activity isn't just noise; it's the digital footprint of Ghana actively engaging with the future of technology. It signifies a growing interest in AI and a desire to level up to maintain relevance in the rapidly evolving job and AI market
